Welcome to Yarrabothula Foundation

Serving the Needy. Empowering Communities.

We are a family-founded philanthropic organization based in Houston, TX and Guntur, Andhra Pradesh, India — serving communities across the USA and India through food donations, student scholarships, health camps, 5K runs, and more. Every initiative is rooted in love, compassion, and the spirit of giving.

Three Ways We Make a Difference

Every action we take falls into one of three pillars — nourish, educate, and heal.

🍲

Nourish

Food distributions, grocery packages and free water supply reaching hundreds of families across USA and India.

What We've Been Doing

From temple food distributions to orphanage visits and scholarship drives — here's a glimpse of our recent work.

Food Distribution

Temple Food Distribution — India

Temple Food sponsored for 300 people at Sri Venkateswara Swamy Temple, Guntur, Andhra Pradesh, India in remembrance of beloved passed away family member, Neeraja Reddy Yarrabothula after bravely battling Stage IV metastatic breast cancer for more than four years.

Orphanage Support

Girls Orphanage — India

Visited Amodini Girls Orphanage with 70+ girls at Kaza, Guntur district, Andhra Pradesh, India and endorsed donations for exam fees, food and daily needs. You can donate too on behalf of your loved ones for their birthdays, anniversaries etc.

Temple Sponsorship

Temple Roof Sponsorship — India

Sri Parameswara Swamy Temple, Prathipadu, Guntur district, Andhra Pradesh, India need sponsors for covering back open area with metal roof which costs at a cost of approx. Rs. 4,00,000. Sponsors names are permanently displayed on the temple premises.
