Orphanage Support

Girls Orphanage — India

Visited the Amodini Girls Orphanage at Kaza, Guntur district, Andhra Pradesh, India — home to more than 70 orphan girls and 15 staff. Discussed donation opportunities and endorsed contributions immediately and for future dates. You can donate on behalf of your loved ones for birthdays, anniversaries, and special occasions for meals sponsorships, school exam fees, food supplies and other needs.

Exam Fee Sponsorship — India

We provided help for a noble cause by contributing examination fees for orphan girls at the Amodini Girls Orphanage, Kaza, Guntur district, Andhra Pradesh, India. Temple Sponsorship

Temple Roof Sponsorship — India

Sri Parameswara Swamy Temple, Prathipadu, Guntur district, Andhra Pradesh, India need sponsors to cover the temple premises back open area with a metal roof as other 3 sides, at a cost of approx. Rs. 4,00,000. We are co-sponsoring a part and invite others to contribute Rs. 50,000, Rs. 1,00,000 or more. Sponsors names will be permanently displayed on the temple premises wall.

Free Water Supply — India

Ongoing clean water supply initiative serving 100,000+ people in India. Yarrabothula Foundation helped to provide free water supply to residents of the Guntur area, Andhra Pradesh, India. The Free Water Supply Initiative is a transformative effort addressing water scarcity, offering vital relief to vulnerable communities. With a commitment to equitable access, the initiative provides clean water, aiming to eradicate the challenges faced by those in need collaborating with local authorities.

Health Camp, Blood Donation & CPR

Save lives by participating in health camps, giving a blood donation, or getting certified in CPR. Give the gift of life right in your community with educational programs, and hands-on training..
