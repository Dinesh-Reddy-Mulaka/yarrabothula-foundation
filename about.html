About the Foundation

A Family's Legacy of Giving Back

The Yarrabothula Foundation is a non-profit, servicing from Houston, Texas. We were serving the community in honor of departed family members of Yarrabothula whose love, courage, and compassion defined in life.

Established to serve communities across the USA, India, and beyond, we are involved in promoting breast cancer awareness, community services, educational assistance, and cultural activities. The Trust is open, transparent, and community-first in all its structure and functions.

Our core mission is to serve thousands of people throughout the globe honoring family's legacy through every meal served, every scholarship awarded, and every life touched.

Our Core

Our Values & Objectives

Driven by Love & Purpose

Our mission is to fight breast cancer, hunger, and inequality empowering youth, promoting education and social welfare, and providing food and healthcare support. We honor departed members of Yarrabothula family by serving communities in the USA, India, and beyond with transparency and a family's heart.

See Our Programs

A World Touched by Compassion

Our vision is to build a world where every individual — regardless of background — has access to food, education, healthcare, and clean water. A world where cancer research is funded enough to save every Bujji, and no child goes without a chance to succeed.

Tangible, Measurable Impact

Fund 500+ cancer donations via BCRF, award 100 scholarships annually, serve 5,000 meals monthly, provide clean water to 100,000+ people, plant 50,000 trees, and support 200+ differently-abled students — all with full transparency and community accountability.

Our Story

How We Came to Be

From a family's grief came a commitment to serve — the story of how the Yarrabothula Foundation was born.

🌱

2022–2023

The Yarrabothula family began informal community efforts — food drives, water supply initiatives, and health camps — in Andhra Pradesh and Texas, laying the groundwork for what was to come.

💙

August 2025

departed family members of Yarrabothula passed away on August 23, 2025 after a four-year battle with Stage IV metastatic breast cancer. Her courage and love inspired the formal founding of the Foundation in her honor.

🌻

2025 – Present

The Yarrabothula Foundation was formally registered in Austin, Texas. The Walk for Neeraja 5K, Neeraja Memorial Scholarship, and monthly food drives launched — reaching communities across the USA and India.
