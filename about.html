About the Foundation

A Family's Legacy of Giving Back

The Yarrabothula Foundation is a non-profit, servicing from Houston, Texas and Guntur, Andhra Pradesh, India. We were serving the community in honor of departed family members of Yarrabothula whose love, courage, and compassion defined in life.

Established to serve communities across the USA, India, and beyond, we are involved in promoting breast cancer awareness, community services, educational assistance, and cultural activities. The Foundation is open, transparent, and community-first in all its structure and functions.

Our core mission is to serve thousands of people throughout the globe honoring family's legacy through every meal served, every scholarship awarded, and every life touched.

Our Values & Objectives

Driven by Love & Purpose

Our mission is to fight breast cancer, hunger, and inequality empowering youth, promoting education and social welfare, and providing food and healthcare support. We honor departed members of Yarrabothula family by serving communities in the USA, India, and beyond with transparency and a family's heart.

A World Touched by Compassion

Our vision is to build a world where every individual — regardless of background — has access to food, education, healthcare, and clean water. A world where cancer research is funded enough to save every patient, and no life goes without a chance to succeed.

Tangible, Measurable Impact

Fund 50+ cancer donations via BCRF, award 10 scholarships annually, serve 5,000+ meals, provide clean water to 40,000+ people, plant 5,000+ trees, and support 200+ differently-abled students — all with full transparency and community accountability.

Our Story

How We Came to Be

From a family's grief came a commitment to serve — the story of how the Yarrabothula Foundation was born.

🌱

2025 & Before

The Yarrabothula family began informal community efforts — food drives, education support, orphans support, water supply initiatives, and health camps — in Guntur, Andhra Pradesh, India and Houston, Texas, USA and other areas, laying the groundwork for what was to come.

💙

2025-2026

Srinivasa's beloved wife Neeraja Reddy Yarrabothula, passed away peacefully on August 23, 2025, in Houston, Texas, surrounded by family, at the age of 56, after bravely battling Stage IV metastatic breast cancer for more than four years. Though words can never fully capture the warmth and fierce love she gave so freely, her family and friends honor her life with deep gratitude and enduring affection. She is survived by her husband, her loving daughters. Her courage and love inspired the formal launching of the Yarrabothula Foundation in her honor.

🌻

2026 – Present

The Yarrabothula Foundation was operating from Houston, Texas and Guntur, Andhra Pradesh, India. The Yarrabothula Neeraja Memorial Scholarship, Walk for Neeraja 5K, Education support, Food and Health drives launched reaching communities across the USA and India.
