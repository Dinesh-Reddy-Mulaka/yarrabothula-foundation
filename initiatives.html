Yarrabothula Neeraja Memorial Scholarship Program

Annual scholarships awarded to deserving students in the USA and India in memory of Yarrabothula Neeraja — a woman who believed deeply in the power of education. Recipients receive financial support, mentorship, and school supplies. Over 100 stuffed backpacks donated since 2025.
